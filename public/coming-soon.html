Core Systems

Everything you need to run a competitive airline from the ground up

✈
Fleet Management
Buy or lease aircraft and keep them flying. Schedule A/C/D maintenance checks, assign registrations, and squeeze the most out of every airframe.
🌐
Route Network
Open routes between real-world airports with realistic airway routing. A gravity-based demand model, yield, competition and seasonality guide every decision.
🎨
Airline Branding
Design your identity — pick your colours and a procedurally generated logo and livery, shown on the map, the competitors page and the leaderboards.
💺
Cabin & Cargo
Configure each aircraft's cabin from economy to first, and allocate belly and freighter cargo, to match the demand mix on the routes you fly.
💰
Financial Management
Track weekly revenue, costs and profit with detailed breakdowns for fuel, crew, maintenance, airport fees and staff salaries.
👥
Staff & Operations
Run your departments — pilots, cabin crew, engineering and ground services. Set pay, manage headcount, and outsource to contractors.
🏢
Banking & Loans
Borrow from competing banks at interest rates driven by your credit score, reputation and profitability — then invest it in growth.
📅
Scheduling & Timetables
Assign aircraft to routes and build weekly timetables with smart departure times and turnarounds. Flights then run automatically on the world's game clock.
📈
Competition & Marketing
Go head-to-head with AI airlines of distinct business models. Run marketing campaigns to lift demand and reputation, and climb the revenue leaderboard.
